# Design System & Visual World: Rahul K Portfolio

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Sophisticated Editorial Design:** Clean layouts, generous whitespace, sharp grid alignment, and rigorous typography hierarchy.
- **Palette:**
  - Light mode: Warm cream (`#F5EFE6`), subtle surface (`#FFFCF7`), deep charcoal (`#211C18`), terracotta accent (`#B96842`), refined borders (`#DED3C6`).
  - Dark mode: Deep espresso/charcoal (`#171613`), elevated surface (`#24211D`), warm cream text (`#F5EFE6`), warm apricot accent (`#E6A071`), structured borders (`#494039`).
- **Typography:**
  - Headlines & Display: **Manrope** (modern, structured) with **Instrument Serif** (editorial italic emphasis).
  - Body & Metadata: **Manrope** (weights 200–800) and monospace tracking where appropriate.

## Quality Standards & Bans
- **Banned:** Generic templates, repetitive floating cards, random gradients, fake metrics or statistics, unnecessary glassmorphism, AI-sounding jargon.
- **Enforced:** Real project screenshots, authentic certificates, verified career milestones, responsive layout scaling, full accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA).
